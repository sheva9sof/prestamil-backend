**Domain:** Mexican pawnshop (casa de empeño) contract lifecycle — silver/general-merchandise valuation, late-payment penalties, amortization display, folio numbering, contract replacement, offline reference
**Researched:** 2026-07-02
**Confidence:** MEDIUM (regulatory findings MEDIUM–HIGH, verified across multiple sources; domain/business-practice findings MEDIUM; exact numeric parity with legacy COCAE remains LOW until confirmed against captured screenshots)

## Regulatory Context (Read First — Applies Across Features)

Mexican casas de empeño are **not** regulated as financial institutions (they are explicitly excluded from banking/CNBV regulation per LFPC Art. 65 Bis) but they ARE regulated as consumer-protection subjects under:

- **Ley Federal de Protección al Consumidor (LFPC), Art. 65 Bis – 65 Bis 4** — defines casas de empeño, requires registration of the business and of every contrato de adhesión with PROFECO (Registro Público de Casas de Empeño), and requires permanent, visible disclosure in-store and in the contract of: % of appraisal loaned, interest rate, CAT, storage/commission charges, and refrendo conditions.
- **NOM-179-SCFI-2016** (current; supersedes NOM-179-SCFI-2007) — the operative standard for exactly what the **boleta de empeño / contrato de mutuo con interés y garantía prendaria** must contain and how costs must be calculated/displayed.

**Confidence:** MEDIUM-HIGH — corroborated by CONDUSEF public guidance, PROFECO official pages, and DOF publication references. The full text of the current NOM-179-SCFI-2016 PDF could not be parsed programmatically in this research pass (binary/scanned PDF) — **recommend a manual read-through of the DOF PDF before finalizing the contract template**, since exact article-by-article wording (esp. cost-methodology formulas in the norm's Anexo) was not directly verifiable here. Source: https://www.gob.mx/cms/uploads/attachment/file/659245/NOM-179-SCFI-2016__Servicios_de_mutuo_con_interes_y_garantia_prendaria.pdf

**Key legal nuance found (MEDIUM confidence, multiple sources agree):** there is currently **no statutory cap** on interest rates or moratory/penalty rates charged by casas de empeño in Mexico — several legislative bills (2017, 2025) have proposed caps (e.g., 36%/year or 100%/year) but none had been enacted as of this research. This means the 2%/semana sanción is **legally permissible today**, but:
1. It **must be disclosed** clearly, in writing, on the contract and in-store (this is the actual binding requirement, not a rate cap).
2. Because caps are actively being debated in Congress, the sanción rate should be **stored as configurable data** (`PlazoParametro`), never hardcoded — which is already the existing architecture (`aplicar_sancion_por_periodo` field referenced in `AVALUOS.md`). This is a forward-looking argument for keeping that design.

## Feature Landscape

### Table Stakes (Users Expect These)

| Feature | Why Expected | Complexity | Notes |
|---------|--------------|------------|-------|
| **Cálculo de préstamo por plata (leyes 925/725 o 720)** | Second-most-common pawn category after gold in Mexican casas de empeño; already exists as `tipo_prenda = 4 (PLATAS)` and `cat_subtipo_prenda` (id 7, "Tipo") in schema but has no calculation engine yet | MEDIUM | Formula pattern: `precioGramoPlataPura × (ley/1000) × pesoGramos × margenCasa`, same shape as gold but simpler (no hechura table, usually just ley + peso). **Ley 720 is a historical Mexican coin standard (peso 1936-45), ley 925 is sterling/joyería — both plausible depending on piece type; the "725" figure in PROJECT.md should be re-verified against a real COCAE screenshot, it may be a transcription of "720."** LOW confidence on exact number — flag for client verification, same caution as the gold %Prestamo table. |
| **Distinguish real plata from alpaca (metal blanco/plata alemana)** | Alpaca (cobre+níquel+zinc) looks like silver but contains none — many casas de empeño refuse it or route it to "Varios" at near-zero value rather than pricing by weight | LOW | Edge case for staff training / catalog data (`cat_valor_prenda` description), not necessarily a system validation — but avoid silently applying the ley-based silver formula to a piece a valuador has flagged as alpaca. Confidence: MEDIUM (well documented material-science fact, not casas-de-empeño-specific regulation). |
| **Cálculo de Varios/electrónicos por valor manual del valuador** | Electronics/general merchandise have no standardized weight/purity basis — every Mexican pawnshop (Monte de Piedad, Prendamex, etc.) uses appraiser judgment, not a formula, for this category | LOW | **Already designed and implemented** per `AVALUOS.md` §2.2 — `prestamo = captura manual del valuador`, then `avaluoContrato = prestamo × (1 + porcIncrementoAvaluo)`. Remaining work for this milestone: ensure `PlazoParametro` for tipo VARIOS truly has independent parameters (own `porc_interes`, `porc_prestamo_s_avaluo`, etc.) and does NOT fall back to alhaja logic — matches the "no hereda la regla de oro" requirement already in PROJECT.md. |
| **Sanción 2% semanal por refrendo extemporáneo** | Confirmed legacy (COCAE) business rule and confirmed directly by client (Jorge) per PROJECT.md Key Decisions | MEDIUM | Must decide: simple (2% × semanas de atraso, non-compounding) vs. compounding weekly — **COCAE parity requires matching legacy behavior exactly, not "reasonable" behavior** (per PROJECT.md constraint). Needs a COCAE screenshot of a late refrendo to confirm. Must be **disclosed on the printed contract** per NOM-179/LFPC (see Regulatory Context above) — not just applied silently at the register. |
| **Tabla de amortización/vencimientos "de referencia" (no persistida fila por fila)** | Clients expect to see when payments/refrendos are due and roughly how much, at time of signing — standard practice at Monte de Piedad and private casas de empeño alike (their online simuladores show exactly this) | LOW | Pure calculation from `fecha_apertura` + `dias_por_periodo × numPeriodos`, generated on demand for display/print, never stored row-by-row — this matches the explicit architecture decision already in PROJECT.md ("solo guarda fecha inicial + vencimiento final"). Straightforward once plazo/periodo config (Diario/Semanal/Quincenal/Mensual) is in place. |
| **Folio de contrato automático y consecutivo** | Explicitly required by CONDUSEF/PROFECO guidance ("número consecutivo" is a named required field on the boleta) and standard expectation for any physical numbered ticket in Mexican commerce | LOW-MEDIUM | `Contrato.folio VARCHAR(20) UNIQUE` already exists (format like `CTR-000001`); needs: (1) atomic/consecutive generation (DB sequence or `SELECT ... FOR UPDATE` pattern to avoid gaps/races across concurrent cajeros), (2) confirm whether numbering is per-sucursal or global — multi-branch context not confirmed in PROJECT.md, currently system runs single-server/single "sucursal" cache so likely global is fine for now. |
| **Reimpresión / reposición de contrato con cobro correcto en caja** | Losing a boleta is common; every reviewed operator (Monte de Piedad, regional casas de empeño) offers a paid replacement process requiring official ID | MEDIUM | Schema already anticipates this: `PlazoParametro.cobrar_reposicion_contrato`, `porc_reposicion`, `monto_reposicion` (both % and fixed-fee models supported, matching real-world variance — Monte de Piedad uses tiered fixed fees ~$5-$25 MXN+IVA by loan size, some regional operators charge 2% of loan amount instead). **Known bug**: current discrepancy between what's charged for reposición/reimpresión and what shows in caja/reportes must be fixed as part of this milestone (explicitly called out in PROJECT.md). Needs a `MovimientoContrato` type (e.g., `REPOSICION`) so it flows into caja reporting like `REFRENDO`/`FINIQUITO`/`ABONO` do today. |
| **Identificación oficial obligatoria para reposición** | Universal practice — every source reviewed requires official ID (INE, pasaporte, cédula) to reissue a lost boleta, to prevent fraud/theft-based redemption | LOW | Already have `tipo_identificacion`/`num_identificacion` fields on `Contrato` — reuse for reposición flow (re-verify/re-capture ID at reposición time is a UX decision, not a schema one). |

### Differentiators (Competitive Advantage)

| Feature | Value Proposition | Complexity | Notes |
|---------|-------------------|------------|-------|
| **Reporte/PDF de referencia offline (plazo, periodo, kilataje/ley, avalúo, préstamo)** | Local businesses in Mexico frequently face power/internet interruptions; a printable reference sheet lets cajeros keep operating (or at least quote customers accurately) without the system being reachable — this is NOT something most commercial pawnshop software markets as a feature, it's a pragmatic differentiator for this specific business's conditions | LOW | Reuse existing JasperReports infrastructure (already used for `contrato.jrxml`) — generate a static PDF snapshot of the current `PlazoHechuraAlhaja` + `PlazoParametro` + silver/varios tables whenever config is recalculated, store/cache it (Caffeine cache already used for `sucursal`), and expose a download/print action. No new report engine needed. |
| **Recálculo automático de tablas al cambiar precio base del oro/plata** | Prevents manual re-entry errors when metal spot prices move (a real daily occurrence) — most small pawnshop software requires manual table edits per price change | MEDIUM | Already scoped in PROJECT.md for gold; extending the same recalculation trigger to the silver ley-based table is a natural, low-additional-cost extension once the silver formula exists. |
| **Preventing an interest-rate/penalty ceiling from being silently exceeded if future law changes** | Given active 2025 legislative proposals for a 36%/año or similar cap, storing rates as fully configurable data (already the architecture) means Prestamil could add a soft warning/validation later without a schema change — a genuine differentiator vs. hardcoded competitor systems | LOW (for this milestone — just keep rates data-driven, no need to build the validation itself now) | Flag as a "future consideration," not build now. |

### Anti-Features (Commonly Requested, Often Problematic)

| Feature | Why Requested | Why Problematic | Alternative |
|---------|---------------|------------------|-------------|
| **Automated CAT (Costo Anual Total) calculator/display** | Feels like "full regulatory compliance" and NOM-179 does reference CAT disclosure | CAT methodology per NOM-179-SCFI-2016's Anexo is a nontrivial actuarial calculation (amortized cost including all fees, annualized) not verified in this research pass, and is not something the client (Jorge) has requested for this milestone — building it now risks scope creep and getting the formula wrong without proper NOM-179 Anexo review | Log as an explicit compliance gap in PROJECT.md/PITFALLS for legal follow-up; do not build in v1.1. Continue disclosing per-period tasa de interés and sanción clearly, which is the piece the client explicitly asked for. |
| **Enforcing a statutory interest/penalty rate ceiling in code** | Seems "future-proof" and "compliant" | No such ceiling is currently law — building an enforcement mechanism against a non-existent rule adds complexity with zero present-day value, and the proposed ceilings (36%/100% annual) are still bills, not enacted law | Keep sanción/interés rates as pure configuration data (already the design) so a ceiling can be added later as validation logic without a data-model change. |
| **Persisting the full amortization/due-date schedule row by row** | Feels more "auditable" / matches how some accounting-grade loan systems work | Explicitly rejected already in PROJECT.md — adds storage and reconciliation overhead for a value that's 100% derivable from `fecha_apertura` + `dias_por_periodo × numPeriodos`, and the business only actually cares about the *next* due date + final vencimiento | Compute on the fly for display/print; persist only `fecha_apertura` and `fecha_vencimiento` (current design) plus each real `MovimientoContrato` event as it happens. |
| **Automatic/API-driven electronics valuation (e.g., pull market price by model/serial)** | Would seem to reduce appraiser subjectivity for Varios/electrónicos | No reliable free Mexican market-price API exists for used electronics; overengineering relative to how every competitor (Monte de Piedad included) handles this category — manual, judgment-based | Keep manual `prestamo` capture by the valuador (already the design), possibly add a non-binding reference note field for common items later if requested. |
| **Serialized/tamper-proof physical folio stock (pre-printed, SAT-authorized talonario)** | An older SAT rule (found in this research, LOW confidence, likely superseded by CFDI-era e-invoicing) required pre-printed folios from SAT-authorized printers with a 2-year validity | This rule appears to predate/be distinct from the current CFDI electronic-invoicing regime and applies (if still active at all) to *fiscal receipts*, not the boleta de empeño as a consumer-protection document — conflating the two would add unnecessary compliance burden not requested by the client | The system-generated consecutive `folio` on `Contrato` (already the plan) satisfies the actual binding requirement (CONDUSEF's "número consecutivo"). If the client's accountant flags an actual current SAT pre-printed-stock requirement, address it as a separate finance/fiscal task, not a pawn-contract UX feature. |

## Feature Dependencies

```
Plazos/periodos por tipo de operación (Alhajas/Plata/Varios)
    └──requires──> (already validated) PlazoParametro schema supports per-tipo_prenda rows

Motor de cálculo de plata (leyes 925/720)
    └──requires──> Catálogo cat_subtipo_prenda "Tipo" (plata) + cat_valor_prenda con "ley" como atributo
    └──enhances──> Tabla de amortización/vencimientos (same downstream contract fields)

Motor de cálculo de Varios/electrónicos
    └──requires──> PlazoParametro independiente para tipo_prenda VARIOS (no heredar reglas de oro)

Sanción 2% semanal
    └──requires──> Plazos/periodos configurables (para saber cuántas semanas de atraso)
    └──requires──> MovimientoContrato tipo REFRENDO ya existente
    └──enhances──> Contrato impreso (debe mostrarse como disclosure, no aplicarse silenciosamente)

Tabla de amortización/vencimientos (no persistida)
    └──requires──> fecha_apertura + fecha_vencimiento en Contrato (ya existen)
    └──requires──> dias_por_periodo (derivado de plazo/periodo config)

Folio automático consecutivo
    └──requires──> Contrato.folio (ya existe como columna) — falta lógica de generación atómica

Reimpresión / reposición de contrato
    └──requires──> Folio automático consecutivo (para reimprimir el mismo folio)
    └──requires──> MovimientoContrato (nuevo tipo REPOSICION, para reflejar cobro en caja)
    └──conflicts──> Bug actual de discrepancia caja/reportes (debe resolverse en la misma fase, no después)

Reporte/PDF de referencia offline
    └──requires──> Motor de cálculo de oro + plata + varios completos (para tener tablas correctas que imprimir)
    └──enhances──> Todas las fórmulas de cálculo (se regenera cuando cambian precios base)

CAT (Costo Anual Total) — NOT building this milestone
    └──blocks──> "Full NOM-179 compliance" claim (log as known gap, not a blocker for launch)
```

### Dependency Notes

- **Sanción 2% semanal requires Plazos/periodos configurables:** the penalty needs a periodo unit (semana) that only exists once the Diario/Semanal/Quincenal/Mensual plazo configuration work lands — must be sequenced after or alongside it, not before.
- **Reposición de contrato requires Folio automático + a new MovimientoContrato type:** reprinting must reuse the *original* folio (not generate a new one — that would create a duplicate/void number, which contradicts the "número consecutivo" disclosure requirement), and must create a caja-visible movement record to fix the known accounting bug in the same pass.
- **Reporte/PDF de referencia offline enhances everything else:** it's cheapest to build last, once the gold/plata/varios calculation engines are stable, since its only job is to print whatever those engines currently compute.
- **CAT calculation blocks a "full regulatory compliance" claim, but does not block shipping the milestone:** the client's explicit asks (interest, sanción, avalúo, préstamo disclosure) are satisfiable without it; CAT is a distinct, larger compliance project that should be logged separately.

## MVP Definition

### Launch With (v1.1 — this milestone, per PROJECT.md scope)

- [ ] Motor de cálculo de plata por ley (925/720, confirm exact leyes against COCAE) — essential, explicit client ask
- [ ] Motor de cálculo de Varios/electrónicos con lógica propia (manual, ya diseñado) — essential, explicit client ask
- [ ] Sanción 2% semanal en refrendo, visible en contrato impreso — essential, explicit client ask + regulatory disclosure requirement
- [ ] Tabla de amortización/vencimientos calculada al vuelo — essential for client-facing transparency and matches locked architecture decision
- [ ] Folio automático consecutivo — essential, both operational and regulatory ("número consecutivo")
- [ ] Reposición/reimpresión de contrato con cobro correcto en caja — essential, fixes a known bug plus explicit client ask

### Add After Validation (v1.x)

- [ ] Reporte/PDF de referencia offline — build once the three calculation engines (oro/plata/varios) are stable and validated against COCAE
- [ ] Ajuste de plantilla de impresión de contrato a hoja oficio — can follow once contract data model (folio, sanción, amortización) is finalized, since template layout depends on final field list

### Future Consideration (v2+)

- [ ] CAT (Costo Anual Total) calculator and disclosure — needs a dedicated NOM-179-SCFI-2016 Anexo review, likely with legal/accounting input, before implementation
- [ ] Statutory rate-ceiling validation (in case Art. 65 Bis 7 proposals become law) — keep rates data-driven now, add validation only if/when enacted
- [ ] Multi-sucursal folio numbering strategy — not needed while the system runs single-branch
